1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase P solidifies role-based access control across the IFNOTUS platform,
eliminating staff/customer crossover, enforcing the principle of least privilege,
and locking down tenant boundaries against IDOR vulnerabilities.

Role Matrix & Capabilities:
  1. Super Admin (`superadmin`):
     - Capabilities: Manage servers, infrastructure, provider credentials, staff accounts,
       privilege switching, host terminal, and environment terminations.
     - Safety: Destructive actions (e.g. environment termination) require confirmation.

  2. Admin (`admin`):
     - Capabilities: Business operations — customers, billing, plans, orders, domains,
       and environment remediation.
     - Restrictions: NO root terminal / shell, NO direct server storage writes.

  3. Operator (`operator`):
     - Capabilities: Hosting operations — files, DNS, mail, databases, diagnostics,
       and environment remediation.
     - Restrictions: NO billing management, NO plan creation/editing, NO terminal.

  4. Customer Care (`customer_care`):
     - Capabilities: Support tickets, customer lookup, and MoMo payment confirmation.
     - Restrictions: NO hosting edits, NO file writes, NO database actions, NO environment remediation.

  5. Viewer (`viewer`):
     - Capabilities: Strictly read-only monitoring and overview across the panel.
     - Restrictions: All writes and executions blocked.

  6. Customer (`customer`):
     - Capabilities: Portal access to their own registered domains and environments only.
     - Restrictions: Zero host/platform administrative permissions.

Tenant Isolation & IDOR Protection:
  - Strict separation between `/customer` routes (customer ownership) and `/platform` routes (staff).
  - All customer APIs enforce `TenantService.get_owned_environment(customer.id, env_id)`.
  - Attempts to access other tenants' resources fail with `NotFoundError` (anti-enumeration).
