Mail Architecture Principles:

     - DMARC: _dmarc.{domain} (v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:postmaster@{domain})
     - Mail A Record: mail.{domain} -> 80.241.223.82
     - Auto-configuration: autoconfig and autodiscover CNAMEs to mail.{domain}.
